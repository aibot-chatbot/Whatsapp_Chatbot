How interactive buttons work

When the AI detects 2–3 clear options (e.g. "Booking", "Pricing", "Contact Us"), it automatically generates button labels. With these settings enabled, those labels become real tappable buttons on Telegram and WhatsApp — customers never see the raw tag text. If buttons are disabled, the AI reply is sent as plain text.
