How to Use Visual Flow Builder

1

⚡ Option A: 1-Click Topic Button Creation

Look at the center panel (AI Info Chips). Click + Create Button on any category card (e.g. Product Catalog or Promotions). It automatically creates a WhatsApp button and populates all relevant facts into it!

2

🎯 Option B: Drag & Drop Specific Facts

Drag any yellow fact chip from the center panel ➡️ Left into a button's instruction zone. Or click any chip to open its full detail modal and click + Assign.

3

✨ Option C: 100% AI Auto-Generate

Click the green ✨ Auto-Generate button on the left. AI reads your active PDF and designs your entire Welcome Menu + 3 Interactive Buttons automatically!
