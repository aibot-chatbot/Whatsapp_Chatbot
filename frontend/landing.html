Questions

WhatsApp AI chatbot questions, answered

What is Omnexa?

Omnexa is an AI chatbot for small and medium businesses in Malaysia. It answers customers on WhatsApp, Telegram and website chat using your own documents and web pages, saves every conversation to a built-in CRM, sends broadcasts by tag and collects booking requests. Paid plans cost RM 39 to RM 169 a month.

How much does a WhatsApp AI chatbot cost with Omnexa?

Essential costs RM 39, Advanced RM 89 and Pro RM 169 a month, in Malaysian Ringgit. Every new account starts with a 7-day free trial of the Free plan, with no card required. AI usage beyond a plan's included credit is billed separately.

How do I add an AI chatbot to my business WhatsApp?

Sign up, then connect your number through the official WhatsApp Cloud API or a Wasender session. Upload PDFs or add your website pages, and Omnexa starts answering customers from that content and saving each conversation to its CRM.

Where does the chatbot get its answers?

From the documents you upload and the website pages you add, rather than general knowledge, so replies reflect what your business has actually published.

Can my staff reply instead of the bot?

Yes. Every conversation lands in one inbox next to its CRM record, with tags, status and staff assignment, so your team can read the full history and reply to the customer directly.

Can customers book appointments through the chat?

Yes. Once you switch bookings on for a chatbot, it collects booking requests in the chat. Your staff confirm each request, and the full list downloads as a CSV file.

Which channels does Omnexa support?

WhatsApp (through the official Cloud API or a Wasender session), Telegram and a chat widget for your own website are live today. Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn are in development and are not included in any plan yet.

Do I need a Meta Business account?

Yes, for the WhatsApp Cloud API. Telegram needs only a bot token, which takes about a minute to create, and the website chat needs no outside account.

Which AI models does Omnexa use?

Essential includes GPT-6 Luna and Gemini 3.8 Flash. Advanced adds Claude Haiku 4.5. Pro includes GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro. The 7-day free trial uses one entry model. On paid plans you choose which included model each chatbot uses.

Berapakah harga chatbot AI WhatsApp untuk bisnes di Malaysia?

Omnexa ialah chatbot AI untuk perniagaan di Malaysia yang menjawab pelanggan di WhatsApp, Telegram dan laman web anda berdasarkan dokumen anda sendiri. Pelan bulanan: Essential RM 39, Advanced RM 89 dan Pro RM 169. Setiap akaun baharu bermula dengan percubaan percuma 7 hari, tanpa kad.
