Questions

Frequently asked

Which channels can Omnexa publish to?

Omnexa publishes to WhatsApp Cloud API, Telegram and Wasender today. Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn are in development.

Does Omnexa write replies automatically?

Yes. Omnexa answers incoming messages using a retrieval model grounded in documents you upload and pages you scrape, so replies draw on your own content rather than general knowledge.

Can I schedule broadcasts?

Yes. Messages can be sent immediately or scheduled for later, and audiences can be segmented by CRM tag.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes two channels, one bot and a monthly allowance of AI replies. No payment card is required to start.

How is this different from a shared inbox?

A shared inbox routes messages that a person still has to answer. Omnexa answers them from your knowledge base, writes the outcome back to a CRM record, and sends outbound broadcasts from the same composer.

Do I need a Meta Business account?

Yes, for WhatsApp Cloud API. Telegram requires only a bot token, which takes about a minute to create.
