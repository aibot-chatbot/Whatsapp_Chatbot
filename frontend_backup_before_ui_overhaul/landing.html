Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about billing, knowledge setup, or integrations? Read on.

How does the chatbot know how to answer my customers?
We use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). By uploading a PDF knowledge file inside your bot settings, the system automatically segments and indexes the content. When messages are received, relevant excerpts are matched from your PDF and sent as background context to the AI model so it responds with high factual accuracy.
Which messaging channels do you support?
We support official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, Telegram bots, and WhatsApp (linked device). You can connect multiple accounts and configure different bots for different channels.
What are AI Credits and how are they charged?
Each monthly subscription comes with included AI credits (in micro-MYR calculations). When your AI chatbot processes incoming queries, it consumes credits based on token size and model choice. If you run out of credits, you can easily top up your balance using ToyyibPay.
Can I invite my team to collaborate?
Yes! As a workspace owner or administrator, you can go to the Team section, enter your members' email addresses, and set their access level (member, admin). They will receive an email invitation to register and join your tenant workspace.
