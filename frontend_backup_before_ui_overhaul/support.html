Common Help Topics

Use these starting points to resolve setup and account questions without leaving the tenant app.

Chatbot Setup

Create or review bots from My Bots, then open a bot dashboard to configure its operating pages.

Knowledge Files

Use Documents to manage primary PDFs, supporting website knowledge, refinement questions, and graph data for a selected bot.

AI Model Settings

Use Tune AI to review provider and model settings. Admin or owner permissions may be required for changes.

Messaging Integrations

Use Integrations to connect or review WhatsApp and Telegram channels for a selected bot.

Conversations And CRM

Use Inbox & Chat for customer messages and CRM / Leads for follow-up tracking, assignment, and lead status review.

Team And Profile

Use Profile for personal and workspace information. Owners and admins can manage team membership from Team Members.

Billing And AI Credits

Use Billing to review subscriptions, payment history, AI-credit balances, and manual-review payment states. This page accepts only redacted support requests and tenant-visible support replies.
