A: Standard international carry-on rules: (1) Liquids: Must be in containers under 100ml (3.4oz), placed in a clear, resealable 1-liter bag. This includes gels, creams, and aerosols. Needs to be alert for meetings immediately after arrival. A: Minimize jet lag with these strategies: (1) Pre-adjust: Shift your sleep schedule 30-60 minutes toward destination time for 3-4 days before departure. (2) Flight timing: Choose overnight flights for eastward travel so you arrive in the morning. For westward travel, daytime flights work better. (3) Light exposure: Upon arrival, get sunlight in the morning to reset your circadian rhythm. Avoid bright light in the evening. (4) Melatonin: 0.5-5mg taken at destination bedtime can help, but consult your doctor first. (5) Stay awake until local bedtime: No naps longer than 20 minutes on arrival day. (6) Exercise: Light activity like walking helps your body adjust faster than lying in bed.
